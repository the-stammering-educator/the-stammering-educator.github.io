About

Learning Architect | Senior Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (SFHEA)

I am a Learning Architect working across digital innovation, educational design, research and evaluation. My work focuses on practical problems in learning, assessment, accessibility and professional capability.

I develop and evaluate digital products, learning experiences and resources. This includes moving projects from an initial idea into a proof of concept, pilot or wider implementation where the evidence supports it.

My approach combines educational design, user needs, research methods and data analysis. Technology is used where it provides a useful response to the problem, rather than becoming the purpose of the work.

Approach to Digital Innovation

My digital innovation work starts with a defined problem. I examine the context, identify user needs, develop a focused response and test whether it is useful.

Evidence from users, pilots and service data is used to decide whether a project should progress, change, combine with an existing service or stop.

This keeps the focus on educational value rather than the novelty of a particular technology.
