VoiceCrafter: Text to Speech

Accessibility | Speech synthesis for flexible, multimodal learning access

Project Summary

Multimodal conversion utility for lecture notes and study packs.

VoiceCrafter is a text-to-speech conversion tool allowing educators and students to transform written study materials and uploaded documents into spoken audio with customisable voice models and playback speeds.

The Challenge

Learners with dyslexia, visual impairments, or neurodivergent profiles often benefit from auditory review alongside visual text. Creating bespoke audio versions of extensive reading lists is resource intensive, necessitating automated speech synthesis that preserves natural pacing and domain pronunciation.

Key Functions

  • Multi-format document parsing (PDF, DOCX, TXT)
  • Adjustable speech rate, pitch, and accent selection
  • Segmented playback with synchronised sentence highlighting
  • Downloadable MP3 audio files for offline study
  • Vocabulary dictionary for correct pronunciation of discipline-specific terminology

Interface Overview

Evaluation & Application

Testing confirmed that auditory revision supported dual-coding memory retention, particularly during commuting and independent review sessions. Providing phonetically adjustable speed controls improved learner engagement across diverse study contexts.
