From Problem to Practice

A systematic 7-stage innovation lifecycle for educational environments.

This framework establishes an evidence-informed trajectory from operational challenge to testing and evaluation. It ensures projects prioritise user needs and pedagogical value rather than technical novelty.

1. Problem Discovery

Identify a clear educational, accessibility, or operational challenge.

  • Student feedback and survey analysis
  • Academic teaching bottlenecks
  • Assessment and marking turnaround constraints
  • Accessibility remediation requirements

2. Investigation & Scoping

Examine the contextual parameters and user constraints.

  • Consulting affected students, tutors, and support staff
  • Mapping existing academic workflows and friction points
  • Evaluating institutional policy and technical limitations
  • Reviewing published literature and sector benchmarks

3. Proof of Concept

Build a targeted prototype to test core feasibility.

  • Develop a minimal, focused functional prototype
  • Assess whether the intervention addresses the root problem
  • Review initial speed, accuracy, and technical stability
  • Gather early stakeholder and advisory feedback

4. Educational Pilot

Conduct controlled trials within real learning settings.

  • Deploy with a defined learner cohort or staff group
  • Establish clear onboarding, guidance, and support mechanisms
  • Maintain human oversight and ethical safeguards
  • Monitor technical reliability and accessibility in the field

5. Evidence Evaluation

Collect and analyse structured quantitative and qualitative data.

  • Learner and educator survey evaluations
  • Semi-structured user interviews and observation logs
  • Platform telemetry and engagement metrics
  • Independent peer review of outputs and efficacy

6. Implementation Decision

Determine future progression strictly based on findings.

Evidence directs whether an initiative should:

  • Progress to institutional deployment
  • Undergo iterative redesign to address discovered flaws
  • Integrate into an existing core institutional service
  • Remain an exploratory research demonstration
  • Be decommissioned responsibly if utility is insufficient

7. Iterative Improvement

Refine active tools through ongoing user feedback and regulatory changes.

Continuous monitoring ensures tools adapt to changing curriculum needs, regulatory accessibility requirements, and student cohorts over time.
