CiteRite

Assessment & Feedback | Formative referencing analysis and integrity verification

Project Summary

Automated diagnostics for referencing conventions and citation matching.

CiteRite is a reference-format diagnostic and academic integrity tool that inspects in-text citations, cross-references them against student bibliographies, and provides immediate formative advice on styling accuracy (e.g. Harvard, APA, OSCOLA).

The Challenge

Referencing errors represent one of the most common issues in student coursework. Tutors spend extensive time marking technical punctuation errors rather than evaluating disciplinary argument. Students lack instant verification before final submission, increasing risk of unintentional plagiarism.

Key Functions

  • Automated pairing of in-text citations with bibliography entries
  • Identification of orphan citations and missing references
  • Style validation across standard higher education citation styles
  • Formative guidance explaining exact formatting rules and examples
  • Diagnostic report generation for tutor review

Interface Overview

Evaluation & Application

Early testing indicated a substantial decrease in mechanical referencing errors among students who utilised pre-submission formative checking, allowing academic markers to focus commentary on conceptual analysis and criticality.
